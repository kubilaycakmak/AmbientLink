Match your light

Stop screen sync, pick a reference color and compare it with the light in a dim room. Adjust channel gains and transfer correction, then apply. This is visual calibration, not a colorimeter measurement.

Test color applies to all enabled lights; gains affect only the selected device. Close and use Stop & restore when finished.
